## 2. Delivered instrument

### One signal graph and fixed sampling boundary

One persistent application-level `ModulationBus` owns targets, sources and routes. Its identity survives composition and workspace transitions. SYNTH retains its bounded 48-source/96-route graph, independent clocks, deterministic seeded sources, sequencers, envelopes, manual controls, XY surfaces, macros, explicit one-tick feedback, patch import/export, recall morphing and graph history.

An application scheduler runs the graph and shared resolution at 120 Hz. Ecology samples a detached resolved-value map after every fourth completed graph tick. Its numerical rates remain world 30 Hz, fields 10 Hz and growth 5 Hz. Visual transport can pause while ecology continues sampling frozen modulation values; Life has separate Pause, Step and Reset controls. Manual Step reaches exactly one numerical boundary.

Typed destinations include scientific/presentation controls, `matter.region0..2.*`, `optical.*` and `eco.*`. Search, domain filtering and empty-search behavior are retained. Existing inactive routes keep their stable destinations and diagnostics; new-route selection filters unavailable domains. Discrete matter controls that reconstruct topology remain base-only.

The new graphs route field strength/origin/blend, transforms, particle trails, condensation/deformation, optical path/transmission, illumination and ecology growth/resource/alignment/excitation/mutation parameters through the same registry. Functional scalar, Vector2 and colour routing remain covered by the retained suites and sample patches. Vector3 type support is retained and core-tested, but current application transforms expose scalar axes rather than a registered Vector3 destination. The new integrated graphical witness authors scalar routes across three domains. See the [architecture contract](../15_CONVERGENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md), [shared graph tests](../../tests/test_synth.gd) and [actual cross-domain input witness](../../tests/gui_convergence_probe.gd).

### Procedural and optical matter

All six FOUND compositions, three stable region slots, analytical field families, bounded field blends, fixed-step particles, persistent strips and geometric strand-to-film morphogenesis are retained. The recovered perspective-correct ribbon extrusion and subpixel coverage remain in place. Ordinary field, transform, material, selection and camera changes reuse topology; explicit discrete configuration or Reintegrate operations rebuild it.

Generated films, strips, particles and ecological structures receive a persistent material assignment through a shared optical bridge. The bridge uses the qualified bounded attenuation, dielectric Fresnel, three-band thin-film response, correlated optical fields, dispersion cues and independent analytic illumination. The original source optical implementation retains its optional second shared-mesh film and opt-in Forward+ screen/depth-copy pass. Global mix zero and region mix zero preserve legacy material paths.

Generated matter and ecology use analytical environment transmission. They do not become arbitrary screen-copy refractors, refract one another's transparent layers or implement calibrated spectral transport. This limitation is explicit in the UI documentation and material contract.

At full shared optical mix, the common optical parameters replace the local legacy RGB/material response. Local hue, roughness, absorption and legacy thickness routes regain their visible material effect when shared mix is reduced; common optical thickness, interference and illumination remain effective at full mix. Geometric condensation and deformation remain active throughout. This ownership split is documented in `docs/16_CONVERGENCE_OPTICAL_MATERIALS.md`; not every legacy material route changes the default full-optical snapshot.

Film shading normals follow deformed geometry; thin ribbons use view-facing normals. The original scientific filament material remains intact, while selected scientific aggregates retain their qualified optical proxy. Optical thickness is an authored path parameter, not measured geometric thickness. The [material ownership matrix](../16_CONVERGENCE_OPTICAL_MATERIALS.md) and [material bridge assertions](../../tests/test_matter_optics.gd) define these boundaries.

## 3. Authoring and persistence

F2 opens Matter, F3 Optics, F4 Life and F7 Synth. The common composition picker, Scenes controls and source presentation remain available within the same application. In Life, an explicit viewport-edit toggle gives left clicks to the ecology tool. Elsewhere, source or matter selection owns those clicks. Camera navigation, UI controls and performance surfaces retain their own input paths.

`xenovection.scene.v2` stores exact source identity, source presentation/selection/camera, matter regions, optical and clock settings, the common patch, ecology seed/initial configuration, representation settings and relevant workspace state. Known built-in source descriptions permit exact source reconstruction; imported source data must already be available as the exact external source. Missing fields receive explicit defaults rather than stale live values.

The loader validates before applying a composition. Saves use temporary-file replacement and retain a backup. Complete scenes are bounded to 4 MiB; patches retain their 256 KiB bound. Legacy scenes and original SYNTH patches migrate using explicit source/preset identities. Ambiguous legacy positions are refused with a diagnostic rather than selecting the wrong feature composition.

Undo/redo remains scoped: Synth patch edits, Matter edits and exact source selection have separate histories selected by authoring context. Optical/base sliders, ecology interventions and performance gestures do not have a universal command stack. Scene recall restores authored initial conditions and restarts graph delay, particle and organism histories; it is not arbitrary reverse simulation or an exact runtime checkpoint. Patch Load/Import/Recall reapplies the referenced starting composition unless a compatible Morph is used. Whole-scene persistence retains all domains' initial authoring together. [Unified application tests](../../tests/test_convergence.gd) cover recall, validation before mutation, transitions and source isolation.

Scientific mode remains independently usable. It excludes generated matter, spatial ecology and decorative optical effects from source interpretation. ## 8. Limitations and successors

The delivered scope is a bounded creative instrument, with explicit numerical and optical approximations:

- Ecology is planar with three-dimensional presentation. Fully volumetric numerical ecology, deformable collision bodies and calibrated biological growth are not implemented.
- Ecology observations drive presentation. Observation-derived graph sources and richer bidirectional coupling into procedural fields remain future work; any such input must use a subsequent tick boundary.
- Ecology has fixed-boundary modulation sampling. Procedural particles use fixed integration steps with field parameters sampled per rendered update; their modulated trajectories are not claimed identical across rendering cadences.
- Generated/ecological transmission is analytical. General transparent-layer refraction, multi-hit transport and calibrated spectral scattering are not implemented.
- The target catalogue uses active domain availability, while stable region slots remain authorable within an active Matter domain. It is not a general per-material-instance routing system and does not hide every legacy material control whose visible contribution is currently replaced by full shared optical mix.
- Graph state, particle histories, organism histories and command stacks are not exact runtime checkpoints. External imported source bytes are not embedded in scene files.
- Full optical mix makes the default Spectral Vortex Nursery and Hybrid Matter Orchestra markedly darker than their legacy material views, even at normal gain. Small organisms and thin branches can be delicate at overview scale; close views improve inspection. Physical owner review must assess contrast, optical legibility and artistic usefulness as well as numerical correctness.
- Hosted software-renderer qualification cannot establish TITAN RTX performance or owner visual acceptance. Those remain explicit owner gates.
