## Specific sensory / artistic observations to preserve

- Strong motion should be possible with *thin lines alone*: braiding, coherent counterflow, slow global and fast local oscillations, traveling light, high-frequency but bounded agitation.
- Perceptual optical complexity can be amplified cheaply through animated environment lighting and detailed normals, without literal per-strand ray tracing or high-poly mesh morphing.
- Continuous smooth LUTs turn cyclic fields into expressive optical motion; reversing phase, oscillating frequency, twisting sampling coordinates and changing hue can feel visceral, even vertiginous.
- Prefer ability to selectively combine restrained dark scientific views with extreme saturated showpiece presets.
- The owner sees eventual **CyberSand** use for optional background and overlay visuals. This project should be reusable without introducing neural tooling or CyberSand-specific dependencies.
