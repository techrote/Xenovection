Unified computational matter instrument · Draft/HOLD owner evaluator

LAUNCH
Extract the complete ZIP to a writable folder. Double-click Launch-Integrated.cmd
for Spectral Vortex Nursery, or Launch-Xenovection.cmd for the original source
instrument. Godot, Python and development tools are not required.
Target: Windows 11, NVIDIA TITAN RTX, Vulkan Forward+.
Launch-Compatibility.cmd is an explicit OpenGL diagnostic fallback; it is not
the qualified optical renderer. Keep every executable and resource together.

FIRST EVALUATION — ABOUT 15 MINUTES
1. Launch-Integrated.cmd. Orbit with middle mouse, zoom with the wheel. Choose
   all six integrated compositions (20–25) using the top composition picker.
2. In Condensation Instrument (23), press F7, open Perform, move the XY pad
   and trigger the envelope. Watch strands form an actual continuous film.
   Open Patch to inspect and edit the same routes driving that transformation.
3. Press F2 for Matter. Select a region, change its transform, field strength,
   condensation and Shared optical response. These continuous edits reuse the
   existing topology. Reintegrate explicitly bakes new field curves.
4. Press F3 for Optics. Enable Optical layer, vary Optical matter mix from 0
   to 1, then adjust thickness, transmission and the independent light lobes.
   The generated films and living matter use the shared optical environment.
5. Choose Evolutionary Light Terrarium (24). Press F4 for Life, pause ecology,
   then Step ecology. Inspect, plant, place resources and excite/inhibit using
   the selected tool and left clicks in the visible numerical domain.
6. Open Ecosystem from Life for the planar diagnostic view. Escape returns
   without resetting organisms. Show spatial ecology restores its 3D view.
7. In the source/spatial instrument, F5 saves the whole authored composition.
   Choose another composition, then F6 recalls it. Open Scenes for named file
   save/open and backup recovery. samples/scenes includes all 25 compositions;
   samples/patches includes six integrated and six retained SYNTH patches.
8. Choose Scientific (5) or press G to inspect the exact original source.
   Generated matter, spatial ecology and decorative optics are excluded.
9. Run Profile-This-PC.cmd, Profile-Integrated.cmd and Profile-Ecosystem.cmd.
   Keep their JSON receipts with your GPU/driver details and visual notes.

POINTER AND KEYBOARD
MMB: orbit. Shift+MMB: pan. Hold RMB: fly with WASD/QE; Shift faster, Ctrl slower.
Release RMB or press Escape to release captured pointer.
Life tab + Edit ecology in viewport: left click belongs to the ecology tool.
All spatial ecology tools intersect the fixed numerical domain plane, even
when branches and membranes appear above or below it. Visual depth does not
change where a seed, resource or signal is placed.
Elsewhere, left click selects a matter region when generated matter is active.
In source presentations, click an exact node/edge or drag a through-depth box;
Shift adds selection. F focuses; I isolates; M creates a knot; U removes it.
R resets the current composition to its authored starting state.
F9 exports exact source selection. F10 exports the current performance receipt.
Exports default to %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Xenovection.

TRANSPORT AND HISTORY
Space pauses visual/graph transport; V reverses analytical visual clocks.
Clocks exposes separate filament, sky, palette, normal and displacement clocks.
Ecology has its own Pause/Step/Reset controls in Life. Pausing visual transport
freezes its modulation values while ecology can continue sampling those values.
In the planar diagnostic view, Space controls ecology directly.
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z use the active authoring scope: Synth patch, Matter region,
or source selection. They do not undo numerical simulation history.
Scene recall restores authored initial conditions and restarts graph delay,
particle and organism histories. It is not an exact mid-run checkpoint.

COMMON MODULAR SYNTH
F7 opens Synth. Perform provides XY, macros, envelope triggers, tempo and clocks.
Patch provides a bounded 48-source/96-route graph with deterministic seeded
sources, sequencers, explicit one-tick feedback and typed destinations.
Blank starts a patch. New component -> kind/clock -> Apply component.
New route -> signal -> searchable/domain-filtered destination -> signed depth
and offset -> Apply route. Bypass/solo and Undo/Redo remain available.
Destinations include source presentation, matter.region0..2.*, optical.* and
eco.*. Inactive domains are identified; topology controls are base-only.
Save/Load uses the private patch slot; Import/Export selects named JSON files.
Morph transitions compatible scene/wiring over 0.8 seconds. Patches in samples
are ordinary editable graphs, not hard-coded showcase playback.
Load/Import/Recall reapplies a patch's referenced starting composition and
restarts its histories unless a compatible Morph is used. Save/Open a whole
scene to retain matter, optical and ecology initial authoring together.
Patch edits restart graph phase/history. Reversing analytical clocks does not
reverse delay, slew, particle or organism history.

PROCEDURAL MATTER AND OPTICS
Matter retains three stable region slots, seeded fields, fixed-step particles,
persistent strips and genuine geometric strand-to-film condensation.
At condensation 1, adjacent strips meet. Shell, pores and growth deform them.
Flow topology is rebuilt for discrete mode/seed/count/field/integration edits
or explicit Reintegrate. Strength, field origin/blend, transforms and ordinary
material modulation remain continuous. Selection and camera movement do not
rebuild generated topology.
Set a region's Shared optical response and the common Optical matter mix to
assign its persistent material to the shared optical environment. Mix 0 retains
the qualified legacy appearance. Optical layer is the shared master enable.
Thickness is authored in micrometres; it is not measured surface thickness.
The RGB model uses bounded attenuation, dielectric Fresnel, three-band thin-film
response and independent analytical illumination. It is not calibrated spectral
transport or physically exact scattering.
Generated matter and ecology use analytical environment transmission. They do
not request screen/depth-copy refraction or refract other transparent layers.
The original optical source proxies retain their optional second shared-mesh
film and opt-in Forward+ screen/depth-copy pass. That copy contains opaque
background content, not other transparent strands or films.

LIVING MATTER
One deterministic planar numerical world drives both visual representations.
Rates: world 30 Hz, fields 10 Hz, growth 5 Hz. Shared modulation is sampled at
completed fixed-step boundaries. Graphical frame rate, camera and detail do not
change the state reached after the same completed numerical ticks.
Spatial branches, flattened membranes and trails are a bounded first-generation
presentation of that plane. This is not a fully 3D ecology solver.
Life's configuration picker, seed, interactions, reproduction and parameter
controls retain the six qualified experiments. The planar view exposes their
full diagnostic controls, fields, trace export and isolation.
Disabling Ecology active releases organism presentation resources and retains
the world; re-enabling resumes it. View changes do not reset its seed or tick.
Direct configuration reset/reseed and choosing a different starting composition
do reset history, as labelled. Planar F5/F6 retains its separate ecology initial-
configuration file; use the instrument's Scenes tab for whole compositions.
Ecology is an artistic computational model, not a calibrated biological model.
